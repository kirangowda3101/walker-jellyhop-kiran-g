# AUDIO-BRIEF — Jelly Hop: Fork From Above (Batch 3: sound effects and music)

Build brief for the audio batch, written 2026-10-03 and committed together with `gen-inputs/batch3-audio-prompts.md` **before any audio generation**. CONCEPT.md, STORYBOARD.md and CHANGE-BRIEF.md are the authority for what the sounds mean and when they play; CLAUDE.md's working rules apply throughout. The slice's sound and music behavior is already built and tested with silent placeholders (commit `7a51e0f`, 52 slice checks); this batch only supplies the audio and must not change game logic.

## 1. What this batch delivers

- Six sound effects (SFX-HOP, SFX-LAND, SFX-WARN, SFX-SPLAT-FORK, SFX-SPLAT-SAUCE, SFX-WIN) and one seamless music loop (MUS-LOOP), each generated by the models named in the prompts file, chosen by me by listening, edited only as the prompts file's "Planned edits" allow, and wired into the existing placeholders.
- Logs: ASSET-LOG.md (a Batch 3 section with one row per take), SOURCES.md (the two models, their versions and licenses), a FRICTIONAL.md draft entry. Evidence in `evidence/`.

## 2. Order of work (with one stop in the middle for my listening)

0. **Setup.** A Python virtual environment outside the repository (`~/Documents/jellyhop-audio-env`), with the libraries the two models need. Run on the Mac's GPU (MPS) where it works, CPU where it does not; record which. The Hugging Face login is mine: I run it myself in my own terminal, and the token never appears in the repository, a log, or this conversation. Before generating, test one short take per model and report how long it took.
1. **Prediction.** If my first message for this batch contains a prediction, save it word for word in `evidence/batch3-prediction.md`, with the time, before any generation.
2. **Generate** every take exactly as the prompts file says (3 seeds per sound) with a script in `tools/` that records, for every take: model ID and Hugging Face revision hash, library versions, device, prompt, negative prompt, seed, length, steps or sampling settings, run time, and the output file's SHA-256. Raw WAVs go to `~/Documents/jellyhop-generations/audio/`. Log: `evidence/batch3-generation-log.txt`.
3. **Listening sheet, then STOP.** Write `evidence/batch3-listening.md`: every take with its file path, length, peak level, and leading silence, plus a waveform contact sheet of all takes (`evidence/batch3-takes-waveforms.png`). Tell me how to play the files (Finder and Quick Look are fine). **Then stop and wait for my picks.** Do not choose for me, and do not describe any take as better or worse than another.
4. **Process my picks** with the planned edits only, by a script in `tools/` that reads the raw files without changing them and records every parameter and result: trim points, fade lengths, peak before and after, and for the music the tempo found, the bar boundaries used, the loop length, the crossfade length, and a seam measurement (the sample jump at the loop point compared with typical neighbouring samples). Output OGG (WAV if OGG fails) to `audio/game/`, copied into `godot/assets/audio/` with a byte-identical check like the art copy.
5. **Wire in.** Put each picked file into the existing sound-event map and the music controller's stream, and switch placeholder mode off. No change to when anything plays. The music imports with looping on.
6. **Checks.** Every existing suite still passes (slice 52/0, mechanics, keyboard). New checks: every approved event ID has a stream; the music stream loops; every sound effect starts within 10 ms (no leading silence); muting M and N still changes no game state. Record commands and results in `evidence/`.
7. **Logs and review sheet.** ASSET-LOG Batch 3 rows (every take: prompt, seed, settings, outcome with my reason in my words, edits, where used), SOURCES.md, a FRICTIONAL draft entry, and `evidence/batch3-review.md`. Rejected takes: listed in the log, with the waveform contact sheet as their thumbnail; the raw files stay outside the repository.
8. **Stop for my review.** I listen in the running game (sound on), check the music seam over at least three repetitions, and play muted. **No commit until I say so.**

## 3. Rules

- Generate only what the prompts file lists. If every take of a sound fails, tell me and propose a change; any changed prompt is appended to the prompts file as a new dated note, committed before it is used.
- My choices and reasons are mine; record my words. Do not infer why I picked a take.
- No MP3 or MP4 files; no file over 25 MB; nothing from the virtual environment or the model caches in the repository.
- Do not edit art, the approved design documents, SLICE-BRIEF.md, AUDIO-BRIEF.md, the prompts file's existing lines, or earlier FRICTIONAL entries.
- Sounds must never decide game state; a missing or muted sound changes nothing that happens.

## 4. What still needs a human (me)

Choosing every take by listening; whether each sound fits its event and the pillars; the music seam over three or more repetitions; the music's pause, splat-dip, warning and end behavior by ear; a full playtest with sound on and one muted. Automated checks do not replace any of these.
