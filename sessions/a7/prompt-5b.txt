Read CLAUDE.md first. CHANGE-BRIEF.md (Version 1 and Revision 1) is the
contract. This is step 1 of the approved Step 5 plan: a supplemental
timing probe, run before player.gd changes. It answers the plan's
engine assumptions with evidence instead of guesses.

Files: create exactly one file, godot/tests/probe_a7_entry_order.gd.
Do not create, edit or delete any other file. Do not change
player_visuals.tscn, scoot.tres, land.tres or any existing script or
test. The only command you may run is the wrapper with this probe:
  ./scripts/run-test.sh probe_a7_entry_order.gd 30   (then 60, then 144)

What the probe does:
- extends SceneTree. It never loads or instantiates the game. It
  instantiates its own copy of res://features/player/player_visuals.tscn
  under a stand-in parent, an inner-class Node2D at physics priority 0
  (the way Player sits above PlayerVisuals), and adds a late logging
  hook, an inner-class Node at physics priority 1000, so the hook's line
  is taken after both the stand-in and the AnimationPlayer have updated
  in that tick. Use the pins test's TickHook as the pattern.
- The stand-in's _physics_process performs the scheduled entry calls on
  the AnimationPlayer, mimicking where player.gd will call them.
- Update order (assumption A1) must be observable. On every tick, the
  stand-in records current_animation_position inside its own
  _physics_process, before making any call (EARLY), and the late hook
  records it again (LATE). On steady playback ticks, with no call that
  tick, report EARLY and LATE side by side. If LATE minus EARLY is 1/60
  and EARLY equals the previous tick's LATE, the AnimationPlayer updates
  after its parent in the same tick. Show those lines.
- Every tick, the late hook logs one line with: physics tick, EARLY and
  LATE positions, current_animation, assigned_animation, is_playing(),
  ScootSprite.frame, LowerPivot.scale, UpperPivot.position,
  UpperPivot.scale, the expected clip time, and the expected visual
  values for that tick.
- Entry methods, each in its own run on a fresh instance:
    R1: play(clip) then seek(t, true)
    R2: play(clip) then seek(t, false)
    R3: play(clip) alone (t = 0 only)
- SCOOT: enter at every phase t = k/60 for k = 0..13, and for each, log
  at least 30 ticks (two full cycles). Expected time on tick n after
  entry (n = 0 is the entry tick) is ((k + n) % 14) / 60, and the
  expected cell is 0 when (k + n) % 14 < 7, else 1. Report, per method
  and k, the LATE position on the entry tick, the per-tick increments
  after it, and every tick where the displayed cell differs from the
  expected cell. In particular, for every tick where the expected time
  is exactly 7/60, say whether the displayed cell is 1 (CHANGE-BRIEF.md
  Revision 1, predicted failure 6).
- LAND: enter at t = 0 and at t = 3/60, and log until the clip ends. The
  expected rig values on each tick are land.value_track_interpolate at
  the expected time for each of the three tracks. Report every tick
  where a displayed rig value differs from its expected value by more
  than 1e-4.
- Pause: during SCOOT and during LAND, call pause(), hold for 10 ticks,
  then re-enter with the method under test at the synced time. Report
  whether the position froze, what current_animation and
  assigned_animation read while paused, and the LATE position and the
  displayed visual values on the resume tick.
- Print a summary line "A7 PROBE: <runs> runs, <ticks> ticks logged".
  Exit 0 unless the probe itself fails to run (a missing node or scene
  is a failure). Engine behaviour that differs from the plan's
  assumptions is a finding, not a failure: report it.

Run it through the wrapper at 30, 60 and 144. Fix harness problems in
the probe and say what you changed; never change the scene or clips.

Selection rule. Recommend an entry method only if, at all three rates
(30, 60 and 144), every entry and every resume it was tested on meets
both conditions on the entry tick itself, and every later tick meets
them too:
  (a) the LATE clip position equals the expected time within 1e-6 s;
  (b) the expected visual value is applied on that same tick: for SCOOT,
      ScootSprite.frame equals the expected cell exactly on every tick,
      including cell 1 on every tick whose expected time is 7/60; for
      LAND, all three rig values are within 1e-4 of
      value_track_interpolate at the expected time.
If no method meets both at all three rates, say so plainly, name the
first failing tick for each method with its log line, and stop. Do not
propose changing the keys, the step, the scene or the brief, and do not
propose time offsets as a fix.

Final report, in this order: the one file created; the three runs
(summary line, exit code, log path); assumptions A1-A4 from the plan
(A1 the AnimationPlayer updates after its parent's _physics_process in
the same tick, shown with EARLY and LATE lines; A2 play + seek(t, true)
applies t at once and then advances 1/60 in the same tick; A3 play +
seek(t, false), or play alone, does not advance on its first update; A4
while paused, current_animation reads "" and assigned_animation keeps
the name), each marked held, not held or unclear, with the log lines
that show it at each rate; the swap-tick result for predicted failure 6
at each rate; the pause results; and the selection-rule result per
method and rate. Claim nothing a run output does not show. Do not stage
or commit.
